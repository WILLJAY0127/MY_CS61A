In particular, non-local assignment has given us the ability to maintain some state that is local to a function, but evolves over successive calls to that function. 非局部赋值，是迈向面向对象思维的关键一步：程序可以由一堆独立自治的 “对象” 组成，互相通信，但各自保管自己的内部状态。
In particular, non-local assignment has given us the ability to maintain some state that is local to a function, but evolves over successive calls to that function.
重点：nonlocal 让我们可以保存属于这个函数实例的状态，并且这个状态会随着多次调用持续变化。
👉 普通函数：每次调用完全独立，没有记忆。
👉 用 nonlocal 的闭包函数：跨调用记住状态（账户余额）
The balance associated with a particular withdraw function is shared among all calls to that function.
同一个 wd，每一次调用，共享同一份 balance。wd (25)、wd (15) 修改的都是同一个余额。
However, the binding for balance associated with an instance of withdraw is inaccessible to the rest of the program.
✨ 封装特性：这个 balance 对外不可见，外部代码不能直接读取、修改 balance。
不像全局变量，随便在哪都能改。这就是私有状态。

只有 wd 这个函数，携带了对应的 make_withdraw 父帧。

再次调用 make_withdraw(200) 得到 wd2：新建独立帧、独立 balance。wd 和 wd2 的账户互不干扰。
